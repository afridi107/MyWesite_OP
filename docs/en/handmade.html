Merino wool baby outfit set

A 4-piece outfit set for babies – our own design, crocheted entirely by hand from merino wool in burgundy and cream. Each set is one of a kind. Lovely as a coming-home outfit, for newborn photos, as a baby shower gift or for a christening.

  • Flared top with scalloped edging and a crocheted flower
  • Matching trousers with a comfortable waistband
  • Bobble hat
  • Socks
  • Material: merino wool, yarn certified to OEKO-TEX Standard 100, Class 1
  • Measurements: top 30 cm long, 25 cm wide · trousers 27 cm long, waist 22 cm · socks 8–9 cm · hat 18 × 10 cm
  • Care: hand wash in cool water with a gentle wool detergent, do not wring, reshape and dry flat away from direct heat
  • Made to order – your own size or design on request

As every piece is crocheted by hand, measurements may vary slightly, and wool relaxes a little with wear. All details are securely sewn on – as with any baby clothing, please supervise your baby while wearing.
