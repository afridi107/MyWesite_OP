Himalayan salt grinder

Coarse pink Himalayan salt from Pakistan in an adjustable glass grinder – ground fine to coarse for meat, fish, vegetables and salads. And a lovely pink on the table.

  • Adjustable glass grinder with steel lid (long version), labelled
  • Ceramic grinding mechanism, adjustable from fine to coarse
  • Refillable
  • Contents: pink crystal salt (Himalayan salt), coarse – 200 g (7 oz)
  • Grain size mainly 2–5 mm, dark pink
  • Origin of the salt: Salt Range, Punjab, Pakistan
  • Each grinder bubble-wrapped in an individual printed box
  • Lab tested: a sample was analysed by Eurofins in June 2026 for microbiology and heavy metals.
