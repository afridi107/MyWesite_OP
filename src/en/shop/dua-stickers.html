Clear or white?

Clear background: Only the text and ornaments show – the surface underneath, e.g. a wooden door, shows through.

White background: The sticker stands out clearly from the surface as a white sign.

Example: “Verlassen des Hauses” (leaving home) in Arabic and German on a wooden door.

The 12 duas in each set

Each dua is printed in Arabic with a translation and its hadith source.

  1. When leaving home
  2. When entering home
  3. Upon going to sleep
  4. Waking up from sleep
  5. Entering the toilet
  6. Leaving the toilet
  7. Before starting something
  8. Before meals
  9. Forgetting the dua before a meal
  10. After meals
  11. Looking into the mirror
  12. When wearing new clothes

How it works
